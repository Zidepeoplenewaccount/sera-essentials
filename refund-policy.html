Refund Policy

At SERA ESSENTIALS, we want you to be happy with your purchase. Please read our refund conditions before requesting a return or refund.

When a refund may be considered

Refund requests must be made within 3 days of the purchase date and are considered only if the product is intact and returned in its original condition. The item must be unused, undamaged, complete, and returned with its original packaging and any accessories supplied with it.

Products that have been used, damaged after delivery, altered, or returned incomplete will not qualify for a refund.

Refund request deadline

You must contact SERA ESSENTIALS within 3 days of purchasing the product to be eligible to request a refund. Requests made after this 3-day period will not be eligible.

How to request a refund

Contact SERA ESSENTIALS on WhatsApp before sending any item back. Please provide your order reference and explain the reason for your request. We will guide you on the next steps and inspect the returned product before making a refund decision.

Contact SERA on WhatsApp

Important

A refund is not automatic. It will be considered only after the returned product has been received and checked to confirm that it is intact and meets the conditions above. Please do not send an item back without contacting us first.
